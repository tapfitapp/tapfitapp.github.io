Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026.

TapFit does not collect, sell or share personal data. There are no accounts, ads or third-party analytics, and the app makes no network requests of its own.

Your data

The reps and sets you log stay on your device. The Live Activity and widget read your current set from the same device.

Apple Health

If you allow it, TapFit writes your sets to Apple Health as strength-training workouts with active energy. TapFit does not read anything from Apple Health. Apple handles that data under its privacy policy, and you can turn off access at any time in the Health app or in Settings.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@tapfit.app.
